The midnight wave

The game opens at midnight local time, so launch rolls west across the planet for about 19 hours, from the Pacific islands and New Zealand to the Americas. Each dot is one unlock moment; tap it to jump to that country. Hours below are in your time.

Every country

Tap a row to put it in the big countdown.

Times follow the regional PlayStation Store listings reported in September 2026: midnight local time on 19 November. The United States and Canada use one nationwide unlock at midnight Eastern, so the West Coast starts at 21:00 on the 18th. Other countries with several time zones are shown at their capital's midnight, and Xbox times may differ slightly. Countries without their own store follow the region of your account.

Rockstar can still change launch timing. Check your console's store page on the night.
